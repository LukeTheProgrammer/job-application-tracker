What goes in the slug
  • Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workable, SmartRecruiters, Rippling: the board id in the careers link (ats.rippling.com/acme/jobs).
  • Recruitee, Personio, BambooHR, Breezy HR, JazzHR, iCIMS: the subdomain (acme.recruitee.com, acme.jobs.personio.de, acme.bamboohr.com, acme.breezy.hr, acme.applytojob.com, careers-acme.icims.com).
  • Jobvite: the path after the host (jobs.jobvite.com/acme/jobs). Teamtailor: the careers site host (acme.teamtailor.com or a custom domain such as careers.acme.com).
  • Workday: the whole careers URL, e.g. https://acme.wd5.myworkdayjobs.com/External.
  • Job boards (the name is just a label for the search): Working Nomads takes a category such as Development or all; Jobicy an industry such as engineering; Himalayas a search query; Built In a listing path such as jobs/remote/dev-engineering or jobs/kansas-city/dev-engineering; RemoteOK a tag such as dev or php, or all; Remotive a category such as software-dev; We Work Remotely a category feed such as remote-full-stack-programming-jobs; LaraJobs all or a listing path such as vuejs-jobs; VueJobs remote or all; JobSpy a search query such as senior laravel developer, run on LinkedIn and Indeed for US remote jobs.
