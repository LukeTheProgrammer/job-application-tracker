What goes in the slug
  • Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workable, SmartRecruiters, Rippling: the board id in the careers link (ats.rippling.com/acme/jobs).
  • Recruitee, Personio, BambooHR: the subdomain (acme.recruitee.com, acme.jobs.personio.de, acme.bamboohr.com).
  • Workday: the whole careers URL, e.g. https://acme.wd5.myworkdayjobs.com/External.
  • Job boards (the name is just a label for the search): Working Nomads takes a category such as Development or all; Jobicy an industry such as engineering; Himalayas a search query; Built In a listing path such as jobs/remote/dev-engineering or jobs/kansas-city/dev-engineering.
